Kitchen log — Phase 5

Pick a recipe or type a custom dish, set servings, edit the ingredient amounts to deduct, then log. Deductions come out of your household pantry, and the meal cost is amount used × cost per unit. If any item has no price, the cost is marked "estimated". Recipe amounts are for 4 servings and scale with servings.
